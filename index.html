    What the tool does, and what it does not

    What it does

    • Uses all 15 ratings on your report: the 8 test plan areas, plus Clinical Judgment and its 6 steps.
    • Starts with your Below areas, then Near, and still keeps time for Above.
    • Checks the 45-day retake rule and builds a printable daily schedule up to your date.

    What it does not do

    It has no practice questions. It is not medical or clinical advice. It is not connected to NCSBN or the exam itself.
